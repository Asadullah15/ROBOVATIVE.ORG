Safety Device

NARIGUARD.

A compact, multi-purpose women's safety device built into a custom 3D-printed enclosure: a siren alarm, flashlight, GPS location sharing, a compact utility blade and a pepper spray compartment, combined into one keychain-sized tool.

View Components Enquire

How it works

NariGuard packs several personal-safety tools into a single palm-sized housing that clips onto a keyring or wraps around a wrist with its strap. In a threatening situation, the wearer can trigger a loud onboard siren to draw attention and deter an attacker, switch on the built-in light for visibility, and share their live location via GPS so a trusted contact knows exactly where to send help. The pepper spray compartment and compact utility blade give a close-range option if the situation escalates. Everything is housed in a durable, custom-designed 3D-printed shell built to be carried every day.

What's built in

The safety tools combined inside the NariGuard enclosure.

Siren Alarm

A loud onboard alarm to draw attention from bystanders and deter a threat.

Flashlight

A built-in LED for visibility and signalling in low light.

GPS Location Sharing

Shares a live location so a trusted contact knows exactly where help is needed.

Compact Utility Blade

A small blade tool built into the enclosure for emergency use.

Pepper Spray Compartment

A close-range self-defense option built into the same housing.

3D-Printed Enclosure

A custom-designed, durable housing with a keyring and wrist strap for everyday carry.

NariGuard is a personal safety accessory. Local laws on carrying blades, sprays and alarm devices vary by region — check what's permitted where you live before carrying or gifting one.
