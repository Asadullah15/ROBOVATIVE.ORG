Service 04

ENGINEERING PROJECTS.

Engineering projects, academic projects, technical implementation and system development — support that takes a college project from proposal to a working, defensible build.

What's Included Get a Quote

What this service covers

This is engineering support built around the college project calendar: helping students move from a proposal or a rough idea to a system they can actually build, test and present. It draws on the same robotics, embedded, IoT and AI/ML capabilities ROBOVATIVE uses for its own builds, applied to final-year and academic projects, hackathons, and research assistance.

What's included
