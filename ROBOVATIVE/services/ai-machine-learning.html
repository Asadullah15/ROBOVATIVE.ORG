What this service covers

AI and machine learning work at ROBOVATIVE stays grounded in application: models and vision systems that plug into real hardware, not exercises that end at a notebook. That means computer vision for robots and cameras, applied ML workflows, and AI logic wired into embedded and IoT systems — covered as engineering solutions for companies or as a structured training track for students building the skill.

What's included

The tracks and build areas covered under AI / Machine Learning.

AI Fundamentals

Core concepts and how to frame a real-world problem in AI terms before writing any code.

Machine Learning

Model training fundamentals and applied ML workflows, from data to a working model.

Computer Vision

Image processing basics and object-detection concepts for camera-driven systems.

AI Applications

Applying AI to real projects and integrating it with hardware such as robots and IoT devices.
