PRIVACY POLICY.

Last updated: 2026. This explains what information ROBOVATIVE collects through this website and how it is used.

This policy describes ROBOVATIVE's own data practices in plain language. It is a general-purpose template, not a substitute for advice from a qualified lawyer — have it reviewed before treating it as your final legal document, especially if your data handling changes (e.g. once the contact form is connected to a live backend or CRM).

Who we are

ROBOVATIVE is a robotics, engineering training and prototyping center based at 3rd Floor, Luminary Knowledge Park, Tolichowki, Mehdipatnam, Hyderabad, Telangana 500008, India. This policy applies to robovative.in and any pages under it.

Information we collect

We collect information only when you choose to provide it to us:

  • Contact form: name, organization, email, phone number, areas of interest and your message, when you submit our enquiry form.
  • WhatsApp: if you message us on WhatsApp (including via the quick-chat button on this site), that conversation is subject to WhatsApp's own privacy practices in addition to ours — we only see what you send us directly.
  • Phone or email: anything you tell us if you call or email us directly using the contact details on this site.

We do not use advertising cookies, third-party analytics trackers, or fingerprinting on this site. The only browser storage we use is localStorage, purely on your own device, to remember small preferences such as whether you've already dismissed the WhatsApp popup — this is never sent to us or to any third party.

How we use your information

  • To respond to enquiries about training, engineering solutions, prototyping or kits.
  • To provide the services, workshops or programs you ask us about.
  • To improve this website and the services we offer.

We do not sell or rent your personal information to third parties.

Third-party services linked from this site

This site links out to, or embeds, a small number of third-party services, each governed by its own privacy policy: WhatsApp/Meta (chat), Google Maps (location embed), Google Business Profile (reviews shown on this site), Instagram, LinkedIn and YouTube (social links), and ProjectsBazaar (marketplace listings for our DIY kits). We are not responsible for how those third parties handle information once you leave this site or interact with their embedded content.

Children's privacy

Some of our programs are designed for school and college students. We do not knowingly collect personal information directly from children through this website without the involvement of a parent, guardian, or their school/college. Enquiries on behalf of students should be submitted by a parent, guardian, or teacher.

Data retention and security

We retain enquiry information only as long as reasonably needed to respond to you and, where relevant, deliver the service you've asked about. We take reasonable precautions to protect information you share with us, but no method of transmission or storage is 100% secure.

Your choices

You can ask us what information we hold about you, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete it, by contacting us using the details below.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time as our services or data practices change. The "last updated" note at the top of this page reflects the most recent revision.

Contact us

For any privacy questions or requests, contact us at robovative.in@gmail.com or +91 83410 57568.
